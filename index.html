COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers before you make the call or send the request.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Mad Dog still works like a real street-level shop. Walk-ins are welcome, and the request form is best for bigger custom projects or preferred artist scheduling.

How do I know a payment request is real?

If it is not confirmed through the actual shop number, the official website, or the official Instagram, do not pay it. The site is designed to make that policy impossible to miss.

Can I request a specific artist?

Yes. The booking form lets clients choose an artist directly, and artist pages can prefill the request so the desk sees the match instantly.

Do you accept walk-ins for tattoos and piercings?

Yes. We are Bakersfield's oldest dedicated walk-in shop. We keep our floor ready for spontaneous projects every single day.
