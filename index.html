ABOUT MAD DOG TATTOO

A long-running Bakersfield shop where art meets skin and the focus is real work, not trends.

Mad Dog Tattoo has been the bedrock of Bakersfield’s 19th Street since 1994. We were here before the digital age, and we’ll be here after. We are the city's oldest dedicated walk-in shop.

Specializing in Black & Grey Realism, Elite Script, and Traditional American work, our philosophy is simple: tattoos should look like tattoos—bold, clean, and built to survive the Bakersfield sun.

Services include hyper-realistic portraits, custom lettering, industry-leading cover-ups, and professional body piercing.

We’ve been featured in local news for our aggressive stance against online scammers. At Mad Dog, we protect our client's money and their skin. We don't do DMs, we don't do digital deposits. You walk in, we work. Owner Marc DeLeon wouldn't have it any other way.
