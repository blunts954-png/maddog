THE TRADITION

Rex King is the shop's keeper of the "Old School" flame. His work pays homage to the legendary foundations of American tattooing: bold outlines, saturated colors, and timeless iconography.

Rex believes that a tattoo should be built to last. "If it doesn't look like a tattoo from 20 feet away, it's not a real tattoo," is the philosophy behind every needle stroke.

#OldSchool #Traditional #BoldLines #AmericanRealism

GOOD FITS

Tigers, eagles, daggers, and classic seafaring imagery. Bold, heavy, high-contrast pieces that pop.

HARD NO'S

Watercolor, anything with "wispy" or "invisible" outlines, or trends that break fundamental tattoo rules.
